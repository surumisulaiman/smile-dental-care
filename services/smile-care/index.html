What we can discuss.

Smile Dental Care lists braces, ceramic-bracket orthodontics, aligners, smile correction and composite restorations. A consultation helps you understand which options may be suitable.

What to expect.

  • Explain what you would like to discuss about your smile.
  • Ask about suitable options, the steps involved and possible limitations.
  • Discuss costs, appointment schedules and follow-up before making a decision.

Choose your clinic.

These services are listed for Smile Dental Care in South Chittoor. Please call to confirm treatment availability at Your Dental Clinic in Cheranalloor.

How much will treatment cost?

Costs depend on an individual assessment and care plan. Ask the clinic for an estimate before deciding.

Can I arrange care while visiting from overseas?

Contact the team before travelling to discuss appointment availability and possible visit schedules. Visit our Gulf patient page or Maldives patient page for planning information.
