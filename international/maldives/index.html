BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

A little planning.
A more considered visit.

Start with a conversation so you know what to expect before arranging your journey.

01

Tell us your plans.

Contact the clinic with your preferred travel dates, chosen branch and the care you would like to discuss. Please avoid sending medical records in an initial general email.

02

Understand your next steps.

Ask about appointment availability, the assessment process, possible treatment timelines and cost estimates. Your dentist will confirm suitable care after an assessment.

03

Confirm before travelling.

Confirm your appointment, clinic address and opening hours directly with the team before booking travel or accommodation.

Clear expectations from the start.

Treatment suitability, number of visits, costs and follow-up needs depend on an individual assessment. Travel, accommodation, visa assistance, airport transfers and interpreter services are not included or confirmed. Ask the clinic about any support you need before making arrangements.
