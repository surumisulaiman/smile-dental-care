04 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Make every detail feel considered.

Give the logo space.

Keep clear space around the artwork. Preserve its shape, colours and lettering. Place it on quiet, light surfaces.

Keep it human.

Natural expressions, everyday clothing, warm daylight and familiar surroundings. Avoid staged medical imagery and exaggerated smiles.

Make it easy to read.

Comfortable text sizes, clear contrast, generous spacing and obvious actions. Buttons should feel easy to find and use.
